Getting Around Toronto

Toronto has an extensive public transit system operated by the TTC (Toronto Transit Commission). Whether you prefer subway, streetcar, bus, or bike, getting around the city is convenient and affordable.

💳

Get a PRESTO Card

PRESTO is a reloadable smart card used across TTC, GO Transit, and more. It costs $6 and can be purchased at subway stations or online.

  • Line 2 – Green (East–West)

TTC Bus & Streetcar

  • Streetcars on King, Queen, Dundas
  • Blue Night buses operate 1am–5am
  • Real-time tracking available in the Transit App

GO Transit

  • Trains to Niagara, Hamilton, Barrie, Kitchener
  • Weekend Day Pass: $10 unlimited travel

Bike Share
